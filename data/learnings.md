# DK NFL Classic — Learnings

Living knowledge base for the optimizer. Standing rules are edited in place; each graded week gets a "### Why" note.

## Standing rules (starting priors — revise as graded weeks come in)
- Build the core around the slate's highest Vegas total, especially when it is 6+ points above the next game.
- Cheap QBs facing the defense allowing the most DK pts to QBs are cash locks; pair with their pass-catchers.
- Injury openings (starter RB/WR1 OUT) create the best value: backup RB with lead role > WR3 bumps.
- Sunday-morning reports (Schefter/Rapoport "unlikely"/"not expected") are ~reliable — fade to near 0 and boost the replacement before inactives.
- True GTDs (pregame workout) get mult ~0.5 and must have a same-team pivot; never make them must plays.
